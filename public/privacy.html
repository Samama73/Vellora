Privacy Policy for Vellora

Last updated: September 2026

Vellora ("the App") is a salon and business management application developed by Redwal Enterprises (Salon Chair Wala). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Information We Collect

To provide the App's core features, Vellora collects and stores:

  • Login credentials — used to authenticate and secure access to your account.
  • Business information — such as your salon/business name and your username, used to identify and personalize your account within the App.
  • Operational and reporting data — business records, activity, and reports generated and displayed within the App as part of its normal management features.

Vellora does not collect any information beyond what is directly entered into or generated by the App for its own functionality. We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or data unrelated to the App's core purpose.

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • To display your business data and reports within the App
  • To operate and maintain the core features of the App

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Data entered into Vellora is used solely to operate the App for your account.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely and access is protected by login credentials. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. If you wish to have your data deleted, you may contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

Vellora is a business management tool intended for salon owners and staff. It is not directed at, and does not knowingly collect information from, children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
